Questions worth asking

Clarity before commitment.

From ownership to launch costs, these are the topics to understand when evaluating an Amazon FBA managed service.

Speak With Area71 ↗
Is MPS a training course or a managed service?

It is a managed service. Area71's published description covers product research, sourcing, listing, launch, shipping, PPC and ongoing Amazon account management, rather than only teaching you how to do those tasks.

Will I own the Amazon account and brand?

Area71 states that MPS clients retain ownership of their Amazon Seller Account and brand. Confirm the legal structure, access, responsibilities and exit terms in the contract before joining.

Do I need prior Amazon FBA experience?

Area71's MPS program is presented as suitable for beginners as well as existing business owners. You will still need to understand your budget, approve key choices and review business performance.

How much investment is required?

That depends on the product, order quantity, samples, manufacturing, freight, Amazon fees, advertising, any business setup costs and the MPS service arrangement. Request a personalized cost outline rather than assuming a fixed launch budget.

What is covered during the one year?

Area71 describes one year of operational support including account management and PPC. Ask for the exact deliverables, reporting cadence, account permissions, service exclusions and renewal or exit provisions.

What decisions will I still make as the owner?

You remain responsible for the investment and key business approvals, including product selection, production budgets and major changes in strategy. The scope of Area71's day-to-day responsibilities is specified in your MPS agreement.

Are sales, profit margins or returns guaranteed?

No particular sales volume or investment return is promised on this page. Amazon outcomes depend on demand, competition, pricing, reviews, fees, advertising and operating decisions. Any projections should be evaluated as scenarios, not guarantees.
